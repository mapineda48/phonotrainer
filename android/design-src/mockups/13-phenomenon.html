Flapping

t/d processesSafe to produce · universal

A t or d between vowels becomes one very quick tap of the tongue: /ˈwɔtɚ/ → [ˈwɔɾɚ].

How to hear it

“water” sounds like “wadder”, “get it” like “geddit”. Not before a stressed syllable: “attack” keeps a full t.

For Spanish speakers: the flap is the single r of Spanish “pero”. You already have this sound.
