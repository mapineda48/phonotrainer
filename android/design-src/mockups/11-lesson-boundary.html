3Why it changes

Palatalization

A final t, d, s or z melts into a following “you”: “did you” → “didja”, “got you” → “gotcha”. /dɪd ju/ → [ˈdɪdʒə]

Safe to produce · universal

Universal: you hear it in careful and casual speech alike, so it is safe to copy.
